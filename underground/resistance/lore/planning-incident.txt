[INCIDENT REPORT / UNDERGROUND]
Subject: Fake 404 route discovered during a student sprint

Observation:
A trap page was placed at a likely 404 entry point. The bot reported it immediately. No student noticed the suspicious path before the end of the year.

Lesson:
The class did not fail because the trap was clever. The class failed because no one wrote a card for "verify the route we are about to trust." 

Corrective action:
Every plan now includes one card for verification, one card for evidence, and one card for a backup path.
