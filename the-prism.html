The PRISM

Residue:

Make growth visible from a first task to field leadership

  • Use nine ordered bands to show increasing capability, ownership, and impact.
  • Use INFRARED through YELLOW for increasingly complex making and cognition.
  • Use GREEN for independent end-to-end delivery and the ship-it pivot.
  • Use BLUE through ULTRAVIOLET for increasing process, project, program, organization, and field ownership.
  • Bank observable work in Module Zero so evidence accumulates across courses.
  • Use the ladder to orient learners and audiences, not to restrict public information.
  • Keep progression visible through roles, scope, evidence, prerequisites, and next steps.

Evaporated: course examples, exact role labels, interface behavior, visual ornament, and local implementation detail; function — curriculum placement, illustration, navigation, and presentation.

Operative sentence: masthead thesis, sentence 1 of 1, main clause.

Finding: above floor as a capability-and-ownership framework · erosion direction expansive · the GREEN lens change is load-bearing · evidence accumulation is load-bearing · course placement and advancement criteria remain locally defined · clearance must not be mistaken for access control.

This is a finding, not a draft.

How to read the spectrum

One ladder uses two lenses. Capability rises through authentic work; the meaning of “more” changes at GREEN.

INFRARED → YELLOW

Warm bands · makers

What you can build. Bloom's cognitive lens rises from remembering through evaluating complex systems.

GREEN

The ship-it pivot

What you can deliver. GREEN means owning and operating one deployed product end-to-end.

BLUE → ULTRAVIOLET

Cool bands · orchestrators

What you can coordinate. The lens changes from cognition to process, project, program, organization, and field scope.
