Flat corporate button; use `gold` at most once per view (the one glint rule).

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" onClick={go}>File compliance report</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" size="sm">Defer</Button>
```

Variants: primary (navy), secondary (hairline), ghost, danger (PRISM red), gold (accent). Sizes sm/md. Hover/press darken; no shrink, no shadow.
